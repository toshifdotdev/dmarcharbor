## 2. What the Service does

DMARC Harbor reads the DMARC aggregate and forensic reports that receiving mail
servers send to the reporting addresses your domains publish, and presents them to
you as measurements and evidence.

**DMARC Harbor does not send email.** It holds no SMTP credentials, cannot relay
or forward mail, and cannot send or spoof email on your behalf. It does not edit
your DNS. Enforcement of a DMARC policy is performed by the receiving mail servers
of your recipients, applying the DNS records that you publish and control. DMARC
is the standard defined in RFC 7489 and required by Gmail, Yahoo and Microsoft for
bulk senders.
