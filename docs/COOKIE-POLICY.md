## Why we do not ask for consent

Under the ePrivacy rules that apply in the UK and EU, cookies that are strictly
necessary for a service the user has asked for do not require consent. A session
cookie that keeps you signed in is the clearest example: without it, signing in
does not work.

We would rather tell you what we set and why than show you a consent dialog with
two buttons and no real choice.

## The session cookie

`better-auth.session_token` is set when you sign in and cleared when you sign out.

It stores an identifier that points at your session on our server. It does **not**
contain your email address, your password, or any of your report data. The
identifier is signed, so it cannot be altered to become someone else's session.

It is sent over HTTPS only, marked `HttpOnly` so that JavaScript on the page
cannot read it, and marked `SameSite=Lax` so it is not attached to
cross-site requests.

You can clear it at any time by signing out, or by clearing cookies for our
domain in your browser. Either way you will need to sign in again.

## Cookies we do not use

We do not set, and do not permit, any of the following:

- Advertising or cross-site tracking cookies
- Analytics cookies such as Google Analytics, Plausible or Mixpanel
- Social media tracking or embedded third-party content
- Session replay or heatmap tools

The public Trust Center, share pages and verification pages set no cookies at all.
If you have never signed in, we have no cookie for you.
