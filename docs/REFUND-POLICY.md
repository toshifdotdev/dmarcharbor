## Summary

We offer a **30-day money-back guarantee** on every purchase. After 30 days we do
not ordinarily refund, but we still refund in the cases listed under "Discretionary
refunds", because those are the ones where not refunding would be wrong.

Most purchases are made through an online reseller that handles refunds on our
behalf. Where that applies, requests go to them rather than to us, and they hold
the decision. We tell you which applies before you buy.

## Who handles your refund

| You paid in | Through | Requests go to |
|---|---|---|
| Indian rupees | Razorpay | **DMARC Harbor**, at `[SUPPORT EMAIL]` |
| Other currencies | Paddle | **Paddle**, from your subscription or billing portal |

Paddle acts as merchant of record for payments made through them, which means
they are the seller of record for that order and they authorise refunds. We cannot
process a refund for an order you placed with them.

## 30-day money-back guarantee

If you are not satisfied, tell us within 30 days of the order date and we will
refund the amount you paid.

- The 30 days run from the order date, not from the day you first used the
  product.
- This applies to monthly and annual subscriptions alike.
- You do not need to give a reason.
- The guarantee is also shown to you at the point of purchase, so you are
  agreeing to something you have read.

## Subscriptions already paid for

**Cancelling does not refund.** Cancelling stops the next renewal and you keep
access until the end of the period you have already paid for. We do not
reimburse the remainder of a period that has not yet been used, because we have
already provided the service for it.

**Downgrades are not refunds.** If you move to a smaller plan, the change applies
at the end of your current period. If we consider a refund of the difference, we
will decide that case by case and say so.

**Upgrades are charged immediately.** When you upgrade, we bill the difference
straight away so you have the new plan now. That charge is not refundable except
under the 30-day guarantee above.

## Discretionary refunds

We will refund outside the 30-day window where one of these applies:

- **We caused it.** A material defect, a sustained outage in a paid feature, or an
  error on our part that cost you money.
- **We changed the product materially** and the change removed something you were
  paying for.
- **Duplicate or double charges.**
- **We were legally unable to deliver**, for example because a payment provider
  account was closed or suspended.
- **Compassionate circumstances**, considered case by case.

A refund under this section is at our discretion, and we will tell you when you
can expect the money.

## How long a refund takes

Once approved, refunds are returned by the payment processor, not by us, and the
timing is theirs:

- **Razorpay refunds to your bank account:** typically 3 to 5 business days once
  we authorise it.
- **Paddle refunds:** typically 5 to 10 business days, back to the original payment
  method.

You will see a refund confirmation from your bank separately from ours.

## Failed payments and the grace period

If a payment fails, we do not refund or withdraw access immediately. We will:

1. notify you and retry the payment,
2. keep your plan fully working through a grace period, and
3. tell you exactly when the grace period ends and what happens next.

Your data is never deleted for a failed payment. A payment failure does not delete
your data, and a failed payment does not by itself reduce what you can use. If a
payment ultimately fails, your workspace moves to the free plan and retains your
data, which you may export at any time.

## Disputes and chargebacks

Please contact us first. We will resolve a billing problem directly and usually
faster than a chargeback can.

Raising a chargeback for a legitimate charge carries a fee for us, we lose the
ability to resolve it with you, and it slows down any genuine refund. If a charge
was legitimate we will still help, but we would rather not have to go there.

## Statutory rights

Nothing in this policy limits your statutory rights under the consumer protection
law of your country. If local law gives you a right to cancel or refund that is
broader than what this policy offers, that right applies and this policy does not
reduce it.
