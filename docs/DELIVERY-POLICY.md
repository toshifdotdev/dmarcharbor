## Summary

DMARC Harbor is software delivered online. Nothing is shipped, and there is no
physical product, courier or delivery charge. You use the service in your
browser at dmarcharbor.com, or at your agency's own web address if it has one.

## When you get access

- **Free plan:** as soon as you verify your email address and create a
  workspace.
- **Paid plans:** as soon as the payment is confirmed. That is usually within
  a minute of paying; a payment by UPI Autopay, eMandate or bank transfer can
  take until the bank confirms it, typically the same day. The billing page
  shows the plan once the payment is confirmed, and we email a receipt.
- **Plan changes:** when they take effect is set out in the Refund Policy and
  the Terms of Service, and the billing page says which applies before you
  confirm.

If you have paid and your plan has not changed within 24 hours, write to
`[SUPPORT EMAIL]` with the email address on the account and the date of the
payment. We answer within 2 business days and, where the payment went through,
switch the plan on by hand.

## What is delivered

Each plan includes what the pricing page lists for it. The service is
delivered continuously for the period you have paid for: reports are collected
and processed, alerts and monthly reports are sent, and the screens and API are
available, subject to the service levels in the Terms of Service.

Data reports, exports and documents (for example a compliance pack or a monthly
report) are delivered as downloads in the app or by email to the addresses you
choose.

## Where we deliver

The service is available worldwide, except where the law or our payment
providers do not allow us to sell it. Prices are shown in Indian rupees or US
dollars.

## If delivery fails

If the service is unavailable for a period you have paid for because of a
fault on our side, the Refund Policy's "Discretionary refunds" section applies.
