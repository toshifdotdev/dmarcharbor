## How to complain

If something has gone wrong, we would rather you told us than walked away.

Email `[SUPPORT EMAIL]` with:

- the email address on the account,
- the workspace or client name,
- what you expected and what happened instead,
- anything you have already tried.

We will acknowledge every complaint within `[COMPLAINT ACKNOWLEDGEMENT]` business
days.

## How we handle it

| Stage | Target |
|---|---|
| Acknowledge | `[COMPLAINT ACKNOWLEDGEMENT]` business days |
| Tell you what we are doing | `[COMPLAINT FIRST RESPONSE]` business days |
| Resolve, or tell you it is taking longer | `[COMPLAINT RESOLUTION]` business days |

If we cannot meet a target we will tell you before the target passes rather than
after, and say why.

Where a complaint is about billing for an order placed through Paddle, Paddle is
the merchant of record and is the party that resolves it. We will still help you
get it resolved and will coordinate with them.

## Escalation

If you are unhappy with our response, reply to the same thread and ask for it to
be escalated. It will go to someone who was not part of the original handling.

Email `[ESCALATION EMAIL]` for a second-line escalation.

## External redress

If we cannot resolve your complaint, you may take it to your national consumer
authority. We will give you the details for the country you live in on request,
and we will not treat you badly for using them.

| Where you live | Body | Where to look |
|---|---|---|
| United Kingdom | Citizens Advice | `[CITIZENS ADVICE JURISDICTION]` |
| European Union | Your national consumer centre | European Consumer Centre Network, `https://www.eccnet.eu` |
| India | National Consumer Helpline, Department of Consumer Affairs | `https://consumerhelpline.gov.in` |
| Australia | State or territory consumer affairs body | `[ACCC JURISDICTION]` |
| United States | State attorney general or federal trade commission | `[US AG JURISDICTION]` |

`[TRADE OMBUDSMAN JURISDICTION]`: our nominated trade ombudsman for customers in
`[TRADE OMBUDSMAN REGION]`, reachable through the body listed above.

We do not require you to exhaust this process before contacting us, and doing so
does not affect any other right you have.

## Complaints about a service issue

If the Service itself is broken, rather than your account or your bill, use the
same address. We route security issues to `[SECURITY EMAIL]` and will tell you if
your report is treated as a security report.

## What we expect from you

Complaints are easier to resolve when we can reproduce the problem. If you can
include the domain, the workspace, the time it happened, and what you saw, we
usually can.

We will ask for more information where we genuinely need it, and we will explain
why.
